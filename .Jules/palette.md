## 2025-02-28 - ARIA labels on Icon-Only Buttons in WebPlayer\n**Learning:** Icon-only buttons lacking accessible labels present a critical barrier for screen reader users by failing to communicate function. This is especially prevalent in dense media player controls.\n**Action:** Add `aria-label` alongside the existing `title` tags for standard WebPlayer controls (e.g., play/pause, volume toggle, fullscreen, PiP). This improves UX by offering semantic hints independently of hover capability.
